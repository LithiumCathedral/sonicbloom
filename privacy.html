Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective Date: November 2026 • Operator: SonicBloom Studio

1. Data Isolation & LabMatch Inputs

SonicBloom.dev does not require account registration or resume uploads to browse partner listings or run the LabMatch diagnostic tool. Skill selections, experience tiers, and domain preferences selected inside LabMatch are processed client-side in your browser to recommend matching AI validation tracks and are decoupled from personal identity records. Local browser storage can be cleared at any time through your standard browser settings.

2. Zero Data Resale Guarantee

We never sell, rent, trade, or broker personal information, email inquiries, or diagnostic responses to third-party data brokers or external mailing lists. If you email us directly at contact@sonicbloom.dev, your email address is used strictly to respond to your inquiry.

3. Analytics, Cookies & Conversion Measurement

SonicBloom uses standard web measurement tools, including Google Ads/Google Tag (`gtag.js`), to measure aggregate site traffic and anonymous outbound referral clicks so we understand which career categories are useful to visitors. You may opt out of personalized advertising cookies at any time via Google Ads Settings or your browser's cookie controls.

4. Third-Party Partner Portals

Clicking outbound application buttons on SonicBloom directs you to external, independently operated hiring platforms (such as Turing, Mercor, or Micro1). Once you leave sonicbloom.dev, any application materials, identity verifications, or personal data you submit are governed strictly by that third-party platform's privacy policy and contractor terms.

5. Directory Terms of Use & No-Employment Disclaimer

All content, compensation ranges, and research briefings on SonicBloom.dev are provided for informational and career-routing purposes "as is." SonicBloom Studio is an independent publisher—not an employer or recruiting agency—and makes no warranty regarding application acceptance, contractor placement, or minimum hourly income. Review our Affiliate & Rate Disclaimers for additional details, or contact SonicBloom.dev with any privacy requests.
