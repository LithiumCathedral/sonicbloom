Data Isolation & Privacy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Data Tokenization & Anonymity

All psychometric variables and evaluation choices collected through LabMatch diagnostics are tokenized and decoupled from identifying personal variables. Responses are stored in dual-buffer telemetry strictly for algorithmic routing.

2. No Data Reselling

Candidate metrics are never sold to external advertising networks. Aggregated metrics used in benchmarking reports strictly obscure individual participant identities.

3. Client-Side Control

Local state caches stored in your browser can be cleared at any time through standard browser storage settings.
