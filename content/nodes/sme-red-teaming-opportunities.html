High-Skill Opportunities: SMEs and Red-Teaming

Despite heavy pressures in traditional freelance categories, some of the fastest-growing opportunities reside in highly specialized AI niches.

Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)

  • Companies like Scale AI advertise part-time, flexible roles for Subject Matter Experts to focus exclusively on quality evaluation, rubric design, and standards definition.
  • These roles monetize deep knowledge in fields like medicine, law, or engineering, positioning the experts as high-leverage auditors defining the "gold standards" models must achieve.

AI Red-Teaming

  • AI red-teaming services recruit individuals with threat-model expertise to act as adversaries, probing systems for safety failures, jailbreaks, and misuse scenarios.
  • Case studies detail red-team contractors uncovering vulnerabilities in healthcare diagnostic systems and financial assistants, directly influencing architecture changes.
  • This sector is rapidly scaling, with projections showing growth from roughly USD 1.3 billion in 2025 to over USD 18 billion by 2035.
