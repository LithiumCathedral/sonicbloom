How much has demand for AI skills grown in the freelance market?

Demand for "AI-tagged" skills on platforms like Upwork rose by about 109% year-over-year, and AI video generation and editing categories saw earnings growth above 300%.

What are the working conditions like for basic data labelers?

Data labelers, often located in the Global South, can spend up to 20-hour days sorting through graphic or hateful content. These platforms have been characterized by researchers as "digital sweatshops" where psychological distress is common.

What does an AI red-team contractor do?

AI red-team contractors act as adversaries, using threat-model expertise to probe systems for safety failures, jailbreaks, and misuse scenarios, often leading to architectural and policy changes.
