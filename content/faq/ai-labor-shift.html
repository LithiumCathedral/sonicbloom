How has generative AI affected traditional freelance writers and designers?

Generative AI has caused measurable short-term declines in traditional freelance work. A study by researchers at Washington University and NYU showed writing freelancers saw a 5.2% drop in income, while design and image-related freelancers experienced a 9.4% income loss following ChatGPT's release.

What exactly is "AI training" gig work?

AI training work is a remote contract category where humans act as a teaching layer for AI models. Tasks include data annotation, ranking chatbot responses for safety (RLHF), and domain-specific expert fact-checking to ensure models output accurate and aligned information.

Do I need a technical or coding background to get an AI training role?

No. Many AI platforms explicitly advertise "no experience needed" entry paths for general annotation. Higher-paying roles prioritize deep subject-matter expertise—such as backgrounds in healthcare, finance, or education—and strong ethical judgment over coding credentials.
