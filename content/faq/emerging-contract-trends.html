Why are companies hiring domain experts for AI training?

AI labs are shifting away from basic crowdworkers toward specialized professionals—like lawyers and physicians—because modern language models require highly contextual, nuanced feedback and stringent fact-checking to perform accurately in regulated industries.

Are high-skill freelancers immune to AI displacement?

No. Recent research indicates that even top-performing freelancers with strong reputations are experiencing income drops. As clients increasingly leverage AI for complex writing, design, and translation tasks, high-skill workers face intensified market competition.

What is "squad-based" AI contracting?

Squad-based contracting is a fractional talent model where organizations hire complete, multidisciplinary teams—including data scientists, engineers, and domain experts—to rapidly deploy complex AI initiatives without committing to permanent, full-time hires.
