Affiliate, Earnings & Privacy Disclosures

Clear, plain-language disclosures governing our referral relationships, published hourly rate ranges, candidate data protection, and independent status.

1. How is SonicBloom compensated? (Affiliate & Referral Compensation Disclosure)
SonicBloom is 100% free for candidates and readers. We sustain our research and diagnostic tools through referral and affiliate partnerships with vetted AI talent platforms, including Turing, Mercor, and Micro1. When you click an outbound partner link (marked with rel="sponsored") and apply, pass an assessment, or complete contract hours, SonicBloom may receive a referral commission from that hiring platform at zero cost or pay reduction to you.
2. Are published hourly pay rates guaranteed? (Hourly Rate & Earnings Substantiation Disclaimer)
No. Hourly compensation bands listed on SonicBloom (such as $20–$120+/hr or specialty medical/engineering ranges) reflect gross independent-contractor rates publicly posted by third-party partner labs at the time of publication. Actual hourly offers, weekly task availability, and total earnings depend entirely on credential verification, assessment scores, geographic eligibility, and active client demand at the hiring lab. Passing an evaluation places you into a partner talent pool and does not guarantee employment or minimum billable hours.
3. How does SonicBloom handle candidate data? (Candidate Privacy & Zero-Resale Data Policy)
SonicBloom enforces a strict zero-resale data policy. Responses entered into our client-side LabMatch diagnostic tool are used solely to route you to relevant contract categories and are decoupled from personal identity records. We never sell, rent, or broker candidate contact details or assessment inputs to third-party data brokers or external ad networks. Read our full Privacy Policy & Terms for technical details.
4. Is SonicBloom owned by or officially part of Turing, Mercor, or Micro1?
No. SonicBloom.dev is an independent research publication and career routing directory operated by SonicBloom Studio. We are not an employer, staffing agency, or corporate subsidiary of Turing, Mercor, Micro1, or any other featured AI lab. All candidate applications, technical interviews, identity checks, contractor agreements, and payments occur directly on each partner platform's official website.
